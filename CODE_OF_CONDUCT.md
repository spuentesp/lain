# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in the LAIN project to be a welcoming and
respectful experience for everyone, regardless of background,
experience level, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability,
personal appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, religion, or
national origin.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming. Assume good faith; newcomers are still learning the
  codebase.
- Be respectful. Disagree on substance, not on people. Critique code,
  not coders.
- Be patient. A clear, kind answer to a "beginner" question is more
  valuable than the same answer phrased tersely.
- Listen. If someone tells you that something you said or did made
  them uncomfortable, take it seriously even if you didn't intend it.
- Default to the public channels (GitHub Issues, PR comments). Don't
  take disagreements off into DMs where there's no record and no
  opportunity for others to weigh in.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment of any kind — verbal, written, or physical — including
  but not limited to: intimidation, stalking, following, unwelcome
  attention, slurs, or degrading comments.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit
  permission ("doxxing").
- Trolling, sustained disruption of discussions, or other patterns
  that discourage participation.
- Any conduct that would be reasonably considered unprofessional in
  a workplace setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing
this code of conduct. Maintainers will take appropriate and fair
corrective action in response to any behaviour they deem
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.

Corrective actions may include:

1. A private note asking for the behaviour to stop.
2. A public warning on the relevant thread.
3. A temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces
   (issues, PRs, discussions).

Maintainers will weigh severity, intent, and pattern when deciding.
Single mistakes get the lightest response that will resolve the
situation; patterns get stricter responses.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this code, or
have any other concerns, contact a maintainer through:

- GitHub Security Advisories (preferred for anything involving
  personal information): <https://github.com/spuentesp/lain/security/advisories>
- Email: [`security@spuentes.dev`](mailto:security@spuentes.dev)
  (PGP key on request)

Reports are handled confidentially. We will acknowledge receipt
within **5 business days** and follow up with our intended action
within **10 business days**.

## Scope

This code applies in all LAIN project spaces — the issue tracker,
PR and review threads, Discussions, and any other venue the project
operates. It also applies when a project participant is representing
LAIN in public spaces (release announcements, conference talks,
social media under a LAIN-affiliated account).
